WHY IT MATTERS FOR THE BANDS

The grade bands are drawn but the promotion conditions have no scores behind them. Five of these six measures already exist in HR Maya — nothing new to collect. They are what turns “twelve months in band” into an actual decision.

WHAT IS STILL MISSING

A skills checklist per band, signed by the supervisor. It is the only promotion condition that cannot be read out of the system, and it has to be written with the kitchen supervisors, not for them.

Scores would be reviewed every January and July, alongside the band review. A supervisor who holds his branch inside the budget while meeting the peak floor and giving every rest day is doing the job the roster asks of him — and that is the case for his own promotion.
