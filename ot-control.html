The gaps we are asking to close

Four of them. Each one is a consequence of how the shifts are set, not of how anyone behaves.

9 hours

Rest between two shifts

A man who closes at 03:30 and opens at 12:30 has nine hours, and travel comes out of that. It happens whenever the roster moves a man to an earlier start than the day before.

28 of 38

Men who cannot be given a rest day

Three of the four branches need every man they have on every single day. There is no spare seat for anyone to rest in.

2,766 hours

Overtime every month

1,798 KD, and the equivalent of ten men working full time. The overtime is covering the staff the rota cannot.

45 minutes

Lost at Ardiya every day

The men are needed at 12:00 and the vehicle reaches the branch at 12:44. The prep that does not happen in the morning is finished in the evening, on overtime.

None of these is anybody's fault. Each one follows from the shift times, the headcount, and the transport — and each one can be fixed by changing those.

Rotate forward, never backward

Nine hours of work in a twenty-four hour day leaves fifteen. Whether a man actually gets them depends on one thing: whether tomorrow's shift starts later than today's, or earlier.

FORWARD — A LATER START

16 – 21 hours

Opening today, closing tomorrow. He finishes at 21:00 and is not due back until 18:30. This is what we roster.

THE SAME SHIFT AGAIN

15 hours

The arithmetic floor of a nine-hour day. Acceptable, and what most men have now.

BACKWARD — AN EARLIER START

9 hours

Closing at 03:30, opening at 12:30. Never to be rostered again.

The cycle every man follows

Opening  → 16h →  Closing  → 33h →  Rest day  →  Opening again

The one backward step in the whole week is the one that follows the rest day, and that step has a full day inside it. The rest day always comes after the closing block, never before it.

Drivers: at least 4 hours between two runs, whatever the roster says.

Overtime: capped at 9 hours a day per branch — 270 a month.

Ardiya transport: leave early enough to be on site at 12:00.

Gap figures follow from the proposed band times and a nine-hour shift; they move with the final closing times. The rule itself does not: a later start always lengthens the rest, an earlier one always shortens it.

Everything leaves from Hawally

The accommodation, the Store and the Central Kitchen are all in Hawally. Every bus already starts in the same yard as the food. So the food travels with the men, and five destinations become two arms.

WEST ARM  ·  ONE BUS

Hawally → Kaifan → Ardiya

Out at 10:15 with the Central Kitchen delivery aboard: Kaifan takes its goods and its crew at 11:00, Ardiya its crew at 12:00. Same bus, same two stops, four times a day.

SOUTH ARM  ·  ONE BUS

Hawally → Qurain → Jabriya & 10C

Furthest first: Qurain at 12:00, then back up to Jabriya at 12:30 and 10C at 13:00. Jabriya and the Store already share a bus, so the Store load rides with them.

Rides with the opening crew instead of taking its own vehicle. The bus is going to Kaifan at 11:00 either way.

A driver’s day is two short runs, not one long shift
