2. The Discrepancies Panel

Shows up automatically when a punch doesn't match what you planned

1
When it appears

If you scheduled someone as Day Off but they actually came in and punched, or scheduled them Working but they didn't show — the system doesn't fight the real punch. The punch always wins for attendance and pay. But it flags the mismatch here so you know it happened and can follow up.

  • This panel only appears when there's something to review.
  • It only shows mismatches for your own team.
  • Tap Mark Reviewed once you've looked into it — this doesn't undo anything, it just clears it from your list.
💡 This is for your awareness, not an error you need to fix. The attendance record is already correct — this just tells you the roster didn't match reality.

One dropdown per cell — pick a shift, or Day Off

1
Every cell is one dropdown

  • — (blank) — nothing planned yet
  • Day Off — this employee is off that day
  • Your location's shift names — picking one means the employee is Working that shift

⚠️ There's no separate "Working" option — pick the actual shift name directly.

6. Save Draft vs Publish

These do very different things — know the difference

1
Save Draft

Saves your work-in-progress. A draft is not visible to attendance — it's just a private plan you can come back and finish later. Use this while you're still figuring things out.

2
Publish

Publish makes the roster live. Once published, the attendance system reads it every night — anyone scheduled Working with no punch shows up as Absent, anyone scheduled Day Off with no punch stays Day Off. Publish only when you're confident the plan is final.

⚠️ Once published, an employee with no punch and no shown-up will be marked Absent — so don't publish half-finished plans with blank cells for people who are actually working.
📑

7. Copy Previous Range

Fastest way to start a new plan when the schedule barely changes week to week

1
One click to pre-fill
  1. Load your new date range as normal.
  2. Tap Copy previous range.
  3. The grid fills in with whatever was published for the equivalent range immediately before it.
  4. Review and adjust — someone's day off might rotate, a shift might change.
  5. Save Draft, then Publish when ready.
💡 This never overwrites a locked (on-leave) cell — leave always takes priority.
🚨

8. Emergency Amend

For last-minute changes to a plan that's already published

1
When to use it

Once a range is published, cells still show a small amend link. Use it for genuine last-minute situations — a sick colleague needs cover, someone swaps shifts, a mistake needs correcting after publish.

  1. Tap amend on the cell you need to change.
  2. Pick the new status and shift.
  3. Write a short reason — this is required.
  4. Tap Save Amend.
⚠️ A reason is always required. Every amend is recorded with your name, the time, and why — this is a genuine audit trail, not a formality.

9. Transferring Staff to Another Location

Move an employee to a different branch — some transfers need approval, others don't

1
My Team

Right after you log in, you'll always see a My Team card listing everyone on your team, with a Transfer button next to each name.

If someone's name has a small orange "No roster planned" tag next to it, it means they have no schedule set for today or any upcoming day — usually because they were just transferred to your team and are waiting for you to plan for them (see Step 6).

2
Requesting a Transfer
  1. Tap Transfer next to the employee's name.
  2. Pick the destination location.
  3. Pick the effective date — today, or any date going forward. It can never be in the past.
  4. Add a short reason if you like (optional).
  5. Read the note under the form — it tells you whether this needs approval before you submit.
💡 You never choose a shift here. Once the employee arrives at their new location, that supervisor assigns their shift through the normal roster grid — the same as any new team member.

3
Which transfers need approval?

Moving someone between two normal branches (e.g. Ardiya to Kaifan) needs a yes from the receiving supervisor — they're the one taking on responsibility for that person, so they get to say yes or no.

Moving someone to or from Head Office or a Driver role skips approval entirely — those don't have a team roster to protect, so there's nothing to approve into. It just happens on the date you set.

⚠️ Either way, the transfer date can never be in the past. If you need to backdate a move, contact HR directly — that's not something this tool allows.
4
If You're the One Approving

When someone requests to send you a team member, you'll see an Incoming Transfer Requests card when you log in.

  • Approve — if the effective date is today, it happens immediately. If it's a future date, it's scheduled and happens automatically that day.
  • Reject — you can add a short note explaining why; the requesting supervisor will see it.

6
After the Transfer Happens

Once a transfer takes effect, the employee immediately shows up on the new location's My Team list — with the "No roster planned" tag, since nobody has scheduled them yet.

The receiving supervisor should plan their roster the same way as any team member — load a date range, pick their shift or Day Off for each day, and publish as usual.

💡 There's no separate step to "accept" the employee once the transfer completes — they're simply on your team from that point on, same as everyone else.
