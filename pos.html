Proposal to management · October 2026

One system, from purchase to plate

Replacing our POS-only setup with Maya's own independent system: POS, recipes, stock and accounting, built in-house and run on our own server.

Prepared by Shakeel · Maya Tex Restaurants Company

Open by saying this is about control, not about swapping one till for another. The POS we use today does its job at the counter; what we are missing is everything behind the counter being tied to it.

Where we are

Foodics rings the sale. Everything behind the counter lives elsewhere.

  • Payments and day-end

Outside it, not connected

Maya Restaurant System · 2

The POS does its job at the counter. The problem is that the left box and the right box never talk to each other, so nobody can connect what we sold with what we bought and used.

The cost of the gap

What a POS-only setup hides from us

No cost per plate

We cannot see what each dish costs to make, or which items lose money.

Losses found late

Stock shortfalls show up at month-end counts, not on the day they happen.

Wastage unrecorded

Spoilage and staff meals leave stock without a trace in any system.

Kitchen not linked

What the Central Kitchen sends out is not tied to what branches sell.

Margins drift

A supplier price rise changes our margins without anyone seeing it.

Data on their terms

Our sales history sits on a vendor's platform, under their pricing.

Postings happen on their own

Goods received → stock up, supplier owed

Dish sold → revenue, cash or KNET, cost of food

Wastage logged → expense, by reason

Payroll from HR Maya → salaries by branch

Result: a profit and loss for each branch and brand every day, not weeks after month-end.

Why the ERPNext model

ERPNext is a proven open-source ERP. We follow its structure for items, warehouses, recipes and double-entry accounts.

We do not install it. We build a lean, restaurant-only system, and Maya owns every line of code.
